# ISHIKAWA (FISHBONE) ANALYSIS

## Problem
Poor reporting and communication of issues affecting AAUA students and indigenes.

## Main Causes

### People
- Students may not know where to report problems.
- Some people may not report issues.
- Poor communication between students and authorities.

### Process
- No simple reporting procedure.
- Delayed response to complaints.
- Reports may not be properly recorded.

### Technology
- Poor internet connection.
- Lack of a simple online reporting platform.
- Limited access to digital tools.

### Environment
- Poor roads.
- Unstable electricity.
- Inadequate water supply.
- Security challenges.

### Management
- Slow response to reported issues.
- Poor monitoring of complaints.
- Lack of proper feedback.

## Solution
The IRO system provides a simple platform where students and indigenes can report issues, including the category, location and description of the problem.

## Conclusion
The Ishikawa analysis helps identify the major causes of community problems and shows how IRO can improve issue reporting and communication.
